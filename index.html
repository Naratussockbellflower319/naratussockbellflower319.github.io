Copter Kid

CopterKid is a classic game of balance and timing. You think you can fly? Don't be so sure about that!

This bird's engine is broken, so you will find it's really difficult to climb up but so easy to fall down! If you thought Flappy Bird was hard, you haven't taken on CopterKid!

Fly through the tunnel at break neck speeds trying to avoid obstacles while not folding and crashing into the walls of the cave!

Only the brave can take on the CopterKid challenge. Can you make it up the ranks and claim the title of Sergeant?

Instructions

Tap the screen to climb up and let go to fall... Easy right?
